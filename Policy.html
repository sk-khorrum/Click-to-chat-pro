Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Click To Chat PRO handles user information. Our app is designed with a strong focus on privacy and simplicity.

No Data Collection

Click To Chat PRO does not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal data. We do not collect names, phone numbers, contacts, messages, device information, or any other user data.

No Advertisements

Click To Chat PRO does not display any advertisements and does not use any advertising networks.

No Third-Party Tracking

The app does not use analytics tools, trackers, or any third-party services that collect user information.

For All Ages

Click To Chat PRO does not collect data from anyone, including children. This app is safe to use for users of all ages.

How the App Works

The application simply allows users to open a WhatsApp chat with a phone number without saving the number in their contact list. All actions happen directly on the user’s device.

Changes to This Policy

If the Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page.
